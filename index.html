FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ClaimLogic pricing and features.

How much does ClaimLogic cost? +
ClaimLogic starts at $150 per month and includes the first 10 funded jobs. Additional funded jobs are $15 each, and the job-based monthly bill is capped at $500. Your first 10 team members are included — additional users are $25/month each.
What counts as a funded job? +
A job becomes funded when the first payment or check is received and recorded inside ClaimLogic. Each job can only be counted once — so even if you receive multiple payments on the same job, it only counts as one funded job.
What if I have 100 funded jobs in a month? +
You still pay no more than $500 for that billing month. The monthly cap means the more jobs you close, the lower your effective per-job cost becomes.
Do you charge per user? +
Your first 10 team members are included at no extra charge. Each user gets their own individual login. If your team has more than 10 users, additional users are just $25/month each. Most roofing companies can have their entire team on the platform without paying anything extra for users.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes. There are no long-term contracts. You can cancel from your account settings or by contacting us. Your account stays active through the end of the current billing cycle.
What's included in the base price? +
Everything — job & claim management, customer portal, roof measure, storm tracker, referral program, estimate builder, automated adjuster emails, invoicing, payment tracking, first 10 users included ($25/mo per additional user), and English + Spanish bilingual support. There are no feature tiers or locked modules.
Is ClaimLogic available in Spanish? +
Yes. ClaimLogic is a bilingual platform available in English and Spanish. Your entire team — office staff, sales reps, production managers, and field crews — can use the platform in their preferred language.
