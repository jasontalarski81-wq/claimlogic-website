📜 Written Data Protection Guarantee

1. Zero Data Sales

Claim Logic LLC guarantees that it will never sell, license, rent, or otherwise commercially distribute any customer data, business data, insurance scope data, homeowner information, or any other proprietary information entered into the ClaimLogic platform by its users.

2. Data Ownership

All data uploaded, entered, or generated within your ClaimLogic workspace remains the exclusive property of your company. ClaimLogic claims no ownership rights over your data. Upon account termination, you may export all data, and we will purge it from our active systems within 90 days.

3. Limited Access

Your data is isolated to your company workspace. Other ClaimLogic customers cannot see your information. ClaimLogic employees access customer data only when required for technical support or legal compliance, under strict internal controls and logging.

4. No Third-Party Sharing

We do not share your data with insurance carriers, lead generation companies, marketing platforms, data brokers, or any other third party for their commercial use. The only third parties that touch your data are infrastructure providers (hosting, encryption, payment processing) bound by strict data protection agreements.

5. AI Data Protection

When you use AI-powered features (scope parsing, supplement analysis, etc.), your data is processed solely to deliver results to you. Our AI sub-processors are contractually prohibited from using your data to train general-purpose models. We minimize the personally identifiable information sent to any AI provider.

6. Breach Notification

In the unlikely event of a data breach affecting your information, we will notify you within 72 hours with full details of what occurred, what data was affected, and what steps we're taking to resolve it. No hiding. No delays.

Claim Logic LLC This guarantee is effective as of October 13, 2026 and applies to all current and future ClaimLogic subscribers.
